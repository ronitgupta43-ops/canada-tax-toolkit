What this includes

Federal and Ontario income tax for 2026, the Ontario surtax, the Ontario low-income tax reduction and the Ontario Health Premium.

What it doesn't include yet

  • CPP and EI contributions, so this isn't your exact take-home pay.
  • Other credits and deductions, like RRSP contributions, tuition or medical expenses.

This is an educational tool, not tax advice. Confirm with CRA or a tax professional.

How it works

This page runs the same Python code as the Canada Tax Toolkit on GitHub, right in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Found a mistake or want a feature? Open an issue.
