Did you overpay? Estimate your refund

Payroll takes tax from each paycheque as if you'd earn that much every pay period all year. If your hours drop, you started partway through the year, or some paycheques are bigger than others, too much tax can come off. Filing your return gives the extra back.

Use the year-to-date (YTD) amounts on your latest pay stub. At tax time, use your T4 instead and leave the future paycheques at 0.

Had more than one employer this year?

Each employer deducts CPP and EI from zero, so you can overpay them. Enter your totals for the whole year (T4 boxes 16 + 16A for CPP, box 18 for EI).

What's included

Federal and Ontario income tax for 2025 and 2026, the Ontario surtax, the low-income tax reduction, the Ontario Health Premium, employee CPP, CPP2 and EI (with the credits and deduction they give you), and the Canada employment amount.

Not included yet

  • RRSP contributions, union dues, tuition, medical expenses and other credits or deductions
  • Self-employment income

This is an educational estimate, not tax advice. Confirm with CRA or a tax professional.

How it works

This page runs the same Python code as the open-source library, right in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Install it with pip install canada-tax-toolkit. Spot a mistake? Open an issue.
